About AI Writing Analyzer

Linguistic Pattern Engine: This application analyzes statistical writing markers commonly found in Large Language Model (LLM) outputs versus human writing.

  • Perplexity & Burstiness: Measures sentence length standard deviation and rhythm variability.
  • Formulaic Trope Frequency: Scans for clichéd transitions ("Furthermore", "In conclusion", "It is crucial to consider", "Delve into").
  • Lexical Richness: Calculates Type-Token Ratio (TTR) and repetition density.

Important: These results are heuristics and probability estimates. They should not be used as conclusive evidence of academic dishonesty or automated authorship.
